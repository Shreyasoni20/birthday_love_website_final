BIRTHDAY LOVE WEBSITE

This version is intentionally NOT a 365-day website.

FEATURES
- Floating balloons, bows, hearts, flowers and stars move from bottom to top.
- OPEN YOUR SURPRISE opens a letter folder/letter.
- Close the letter with X and the page returns to Happy Birthday.
- Clickable photo memories with editable messages in script.js.
- KPI section: 800 days together and 1000 days since first meeting.
- Blow the Candles: candles go out and party-popper/confetti animation plays.
- Launch Grand Finale: a large final letter opens, hearts fly first, and the final message is editable directly on the page.

CUSTOMISE
1. Open index.html.
2. Replace the text inside the first .editable-letter with your own birthday letter.
3. Replace the text inside the final .editable-letter with your grand-finale message.
4. Edit galleryItems in script.js for your photos and memory messages.
5. Put your music at assets/music/song.mp3.

The editable text boxes are intentionally blank/placeholder-style so the important words come from you.
